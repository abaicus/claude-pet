HELLO

this is your pet.

It lives on top of your desktop and feeds on your Claude Code sessions. Prompts and edits are food; commits, green tests and deploys are xp. It grows through 25 levels and seven forms.

It only ever watches. Nothing it does can slow Claude down, block a tool call, or fail a session — and it never talks to the network.

Your existing settings are preserved, installing twice changes nothing, and Settings → Claude hooks removes it again whenever you like.

What it keeps: which tool ran, how many lines an edit touched, the file name, the exit status. What it never keeps: a single character of your prompts — only how long they were.

living with it.

  • right-clicktreat, hide, sounds, settings, quit

Keys: ⌘⌥, settings · ⌘⌥T treat · ⌘⌥V hide/show · ⌘⌥M sounds · ⌘⌥P click-through · ⌘⌥Q quit.
⌘⌥P is the escape hatch — a click-through pet can't offer its own way back.

It never lies to you: anything estimated is marked ~, and anything it can't read is simply absent. You can replay this intro any time from the right-click menu.
